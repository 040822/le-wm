\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}
We ask whether joint training improves decisions, which connections account for any change, and what inference cost is required. The six comparisons below separate system performance, training, mechanisms, and deployment cost.

\subsection{Evaluation protocol and evidence scope}
\label{sec:eval_protocol}
The benchmark covers Cube, Push-T, Reacher, and TwoRoom. Matched training focuses on Push-T and Reacher. Extension tasks are reported separately and do not change the core task set after observing results.

Images have resolution $224\times224$ and latents have width 192. Plans contain five blocks of five primitive actions. The controller executes the 25-step sequence before replanning, unless terminated, within a 50-step episode budget.

Goal images come from 25 primitive steps after each trajectory start. Every comparison records its cohort and goal identities; results from different start--goal distributions are not pooled.

Candidate selection uses Euler sampling with $S=2$ and $N=64$. Candidates are clipped to physical bounds before scoring and execution. Each scorer converts the same physical actions to its own input convention.

LeWM encodes raw observations and goals using its own encoder, retains its standard history, and rolls out autoregressively. Its comparison with Joint-B is a system comparison, not an isolated test of transformer sharing.

The ongoing matched study targets training seeds 3072 and 4096 at epoch 10. Completed models, evaluation counts, and data exposure are \PaperResult{protocol-completed-scope}. Additional seeds are reported only if actually completed.

Training, development, and confirmation episode identities are recorded explicitly. A previously inspected confirmation set is labeled as reused; new starts from training episodes are not called training-unseen evaluation.

Effects pair common starts and account for repeated inference and training seeds. Primary comparisons are Joint-B versus LeWM and Joint-B versus B-only; the actual task/contrast correction family is \PaperResult{protocol-test-family}.

Tables with blue TBD entries reserve incomplete results. They do not assert completed runs or positive effects. Dashes identify entries outside the declared comparison; completed negative results remain in the tables.
