\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:limitations}
CoWM couples goal-conditioned action generation with action-conditioned latent dynamics to support selection and refinement under a limited decision budget. Parameter sharing, generated-action inputs, and the action-mediated gradient path connect the two tasks during learning. Parallel future prediction and configurable candidate and update counts provide complementary controls over decision computation. The completed comparisons support \PaperResult{conclusion-supported-claim}.

The approach also has specific limitations. Estimated-action training uses recorded future targets, so it does not provide counterfactual supervision for arbitrary generated actions. Predicted cost gradients may improve the model's score without improving physical execution, and a larger candidate pool may expose prediction errors. Our common-pool and executed-refinement evaluations examine these effects alongside closed-loop success. The remaining empirical scope is \PaperResult{conclusion-unresolved-scope}.

Resource benefits depend on the implementation and operating point. Sharing weights reduces backbone duplication but does not eliminate the separate computations of A and B. Decision latency includes all generation, prediction, and refinement work, while training cost is reported separately. Success--latency comparisons with both small and large search budgets determine when the coupled model offers a useful alternative to extensive online search.
