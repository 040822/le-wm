\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
Coupling is a design variable. Recorded-action multi-task training may suffice; estimated inputs may help without their gradients; or joint training may offer no reliable decision benefit.

The retained configuration is \PaperResult{discussion-configuration}.

The supported training effect is \PaperResult{discussion-training-scope}. Its scope follows complete matched contrasts. Without Pred-Detach or a sharing control, input, gradient, and parameter effects cannot all be independently attributed.

Estimated-action training retains the recorded future target. It may encourage consistency near recorded actions, but it does not observe their counterfactual futures.

Temporal attention constraints likewise do not establish physical causality.

Physical state readability, continuous ranking, top-1 selection, and closed-loop success are different endpoints.

In current checkpoints, continuous ranking gains do not consistently transfer to control and do not replicate across every setting.

The final task and seed variation is \PaperResult{discussion-generalization}. Reused evaluation episodes, training-data exposure, and incomplete contrasts limit the claims even when a particular metric improves.

Latency describes the stated hardware and isolated policy call. Total training cost, resident parameters, history handling, and search budgets affect the comparison.

The validated efficiency scope is \PaperResult{discussion-efficiency-scope}.

Online branch supervision changes the data setting. Its exploratory improvement is reported separately in the supplement and is not evidence for the original offline training objective.

\section{Conclusion}
CoWM combines action flow and parallel latent dynamics while distinguishing parameter, input, and gradient connections. Its evaluation connects physical consequences to action choice and measured decision cost.

The completed comparisons support \PaperResult{conclusion-supported-claim}. The unresolved scope is \PaperResult{conclusion-unresolved-scope}.

The fixed-checkpoint reference shows task-dependent selection value and lower latency without validated CEM replacement.
