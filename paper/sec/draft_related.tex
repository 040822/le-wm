\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Latent world models for control.}
DINO-WM predicts pretrained visual patch features and uses action optimization to pursue visual goals~\cite{zhou2024dinowm}. LeWM learns a visual representation and predictive dynamics jointly using a latent prediction objective and a Gaussian-distribution regularizer~\cite{maes2026lewm}. Our representation learning follows the latter approach. We replace recursive future prediction in the Fast branch with causal prefix prediction and add action and path modeling to the shared transformer. Our local LeWM evaluations use the repository's audited protocol; published paper numbers are not interchangeable with these local measurements.

\paragraph{Generative action and trajectory models.}
Diffusion Policy models visuomotor action sequences with conditional diffusion~\cite{chi2023diffusion}. Flow matching trains vector fields along prescribed probability paths~\cite{lipman2022flow}; we use linear noise-to-data interpolation for both action and latent-path generation. LeFlow is the closest planning reference: it generates latent trajectories, decodes actions with inverse dynamics, and verifies candidates using a frozen world model~\cite{huang2026leflow}. We adopt that proposal--decode--verify decomposition. The object of study here is its integration with an action generator and a parallel dynamics predictor in a jointly trained backbone. Establishing that this sharing is beneficial requires matched controls against separate modules.

\paragraph{World-action models and future information.}
Fast-WAM separates the benefit of video co-training from explicit future imagination at inference~\cite{yuan2026fastwam}. Faster-WAM studies efficient future conditioning and reuses future representations during action denoising~\cite{zhao2026fasterwam}. These works motivate examining when future information is useful, but their video-conditioning mechanisms differ from selecting executable action candidates by predicted latent outcomes. Our study retains a direct action mode and measures the additional value, and possible failure, of explicit planning.
